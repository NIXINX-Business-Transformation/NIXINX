# NIXINX — delivery and internal business model

## Confirmed Vercel observation
Project `sites` in team `nixinx-projects` has verified wildcard `*.nixinx.site` and `nixinx.site`. This is a valid routing starting point, **not proof of tenant authorization or Looker privacy**. Keep old project intact.

## MI delivery architecture — target
`client.nixinx.site` → authenticate person → backend tenant/site/role entitlement in NIXINX Control → private report catalogue / instance → Google/Looker ACL validation → authorized client report. No iframe or hostname-only security. Suspension must revoke licensed visualizations and protected engine access as contracted, without deleting client-owned data or stopping independent daily ingestion.

## Business Model Canvas — working content
**Customers:** SMEs, independent/multi-location hospitality first; retail, staffing, wholesale and services later.
**Value:** implement right-sized technology, reuse industry solutions, translate operations to measurable management results.
**Channels:** .com acquisition, .app operational software, .site MI, .org internal operations and billing.
**Relationships:** project onboarding, automated recurring support/MI, separately billed strategic consulting.
**Revenues:** implementation projects; recurring contracted MI/maintenance/licensing; additional integration/software projects; optional PM/advisory; referral commissions only if partner contract exists.
**Resources:** founder expertise, reusable intellectual property, domain connectors, templates, NIXINX Engine/Control, strategic relationships.
**Activities:** sales, assessments, installations, integrations, release governance, monitoring, KPI models, consulting.
**Partners (candidates only):** Toast, TouchBistro, Clover, Square, Owner.com, Google Cloud/BigQuery/Firebase/Looker, Vercel, POS hardware vendors, installers, cybersecurity, payment/delivery providers, marketing/SEO agencies, accountants and local referral networks.
**Costs:** project labor, subcontractors, NIXINX cloud, software licenses, acquisition, maintenance, professional advisory; customer-specific data/BQ processing billed to customer in actual configuration.

## FODA / SWOT — hypotheses until measured
Strengths: proven operations and implementation experience, repeatable architecture. Weaknesses: founder concentration, MI automation/access control not fully operational. Opportunities: SME management gaps, SaaS partners and referrals, expansion by domain/industry. Threats: API changes, security risks, availability of alternative low-cost SaaS, increasing operating costs, client concentration.

## Commercial guardrails
Do not promise the founder personally works a full-time week for each customer. Avoid describing base recurring fee as unlimited installation or unlimited custom software. Reference compensation experience only as context for value, not proof of future subscription pricing. Keep private internal profitability, referral rates and strategic assumptions out of the public PWA.
